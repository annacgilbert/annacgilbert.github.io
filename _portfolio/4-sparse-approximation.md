Underpinning much of my applied work is a long-running program on the
mathematical foundations of **sparse approximation**: sublinear-time and sparse
Fourier algorithms, compressive sensing, and structured transforms such as
orthogonal polynomial transforms (*Sparse Recovery for Orthogonal Polynomial
Transforms*, ICALP 2020).

This is where the algorithms, harmonic analysis, and probability behind the rest
of the group's work come from — a good entry point for mathematically inclined
students.
