I work on recovering signals from few measurements while respecting additional
constraints — most notably **differential privacy** — and on **inverse problems**
including nonlinear inverse scattering.

Representative work: *Local Differential Privacy for Physical Sensor Data and
Sparse Recovery* (ACHA 2021) and *Nonlinear Iterative Hard Thresholding for
Inverse Scattering* (SIAM J. Imaging Sciences 2020).
